# Product (steering)

**What**

Trustbound Airbag is a runtime safety layer for Solana focused on the moment before a user approves an action.

**Invariant loop**

Intent → Risk Analysis → Alert → Decision → Containment

**Promise**

Plain-language risk surfaced before approval; a clear stop/continue decision; containment guidance when the user proceeds into risk.

**Who**

Solana users facing unfamiliar or high-stakes interactions; hackathon audience judging the demo in minutes.

**Positioning**

Safety at approval time — not a wallet replacement, not a broad analytics or enterprise product.

**Wow**

A high-risk intent is understandable on a phone before the user commits; the demo tells that story end-to-end.
